              Likely duplicate records

              An explainable match score ranks pairs for review. Nothing is merged without a person checking it. Select a pair to see why it scored.

              Model card. Logistic score on six features: name similarity, shared email, shared address, shared phone, matching class year and a spouse link. Weights are set from last year’s reviewed merges. Pairs at 90% or more go to the weekly merge review, 60% to 90% to manual review, and below 60% are dropped. The score ranks work. It never merges records on its own.

              Program management outline

              The program is the operating system around the five projects. It sets the mandate, the decision rhythm, the tranches, the dependencies and the capacity limit.

              MandateOne advancement services operating system across gifts, close, CRM, data, reporting and the team.
              GovernanceSAVP chairs. Director leads the program. Monthly portfolio review. Gate decisions include IT and Finance.
              WorkstreamsFive projects, each with one owner, one next gate and one benefit.
              TranchesProtect year-end through December. Stabilize data and reporting January to March. Modernize and pilot April to June. Prove benefits July to December.
              DependenciesYear-end freeze gates every project. Workday mapping feeds reporting and data quality. Blackbaud releases freeze December 15 to January 15.
              CapacityThree-person operations team plus temporary peak help. WIP limit is five. Close and peak outrank project dates.
              BenefitsFive measures, each with a baseline, target, owner and review date.
              EscalationDirector to SAVP. IT and Finance co-sign technical and reconciliation decisions. A failed gate stops or changes the project.

              Problem

              Workday Student changes how student, program and term data arrive. Raiser’s Edge must map that data to gift designations, pledges, scholarships and stewardship without rekeying or losing the audit trail.

              Objective

              Map and test the student identity, program, gift designation and pledge paths. Run one parallel close before cutover. Leave a documented interface, a rollback plan and trained owners.

              In scope

              • Student and program identifiers
              • Gift designation and scholarship mapping
              • Pledge and commitment records
              • Parallel reconciliation and audit evidence
              • Training for AS staff and campus partners

              Out of scope

              • Workday finance configuration
              • Gift acceptance and endowment policy
              • Blackbaud contract negotiation
              • Institutional reporting outside IA
              • New software procurement
